Move a fee, keep every investment

Moving the 3% platform fee to the company keeps each investment and lowers cash due by £3,720.00; whoever paid the old amount is listed for a refund. It needs consent, can be reversed, and locks the currency; later edits void it or prompt a re-check.

Split the exit, step by step

Carry is exactly 20% of the profit

A £390,000 exit after 6 years with £4,500 of costs: investors get their money and an 8% hurdle first, then the lead catches up. The lead's £57,021.00 is 20% of the £285,105.00 profit; Export holdings CSV and Export distribution CSV save the figures.

Step by step

StepTo investorsTo the leadWhy
Exit costs——£4,500.00 is paid first, before anyone else.
Return of capital£100,395.00£0.00Investors get back what they invested before any carry is paid.
Preferred return (hurdle)£58,919.25£0.008% a year, compounding over 6 years on capital, paid to investors before any carry.
Lead catch-up£0.00£14,729.81The lead takes 100% of the next distributions until it holds 20% of all profit paid so far.
Carried interest split£169,164.75£42,291.19Remaining profit: 80% to investors, 20% to the lead.

Or carry in tiers by return multiple

Tiered carry has no hurdle or catch-up: each rate applies only to the profit inside its band. The last band has no ceiling, and Add tier adds a band above it.

A loss is not an error

Trust the figures, keep the record

Every result checks itself

A failed self-check hides the figures, a blocker holds export and hand-off, needs-review waits for you, and a reminder stops nothing. Money is whole pence, and v1.5.0 passed 551 unit tests and 272 of 272 due test-plan cases.

Checks

✓ Self-check For every investor, cash received = invested + platform fee share + fees + refund

✓ Self-check No refund is negative

✓ Self-check Wire to company + company-paid fees = total invested

✓ Blocker At least one investor is in the allocation

✓ Blocker Nobody falls below the minimum after scale-back

Every decision, on the record
