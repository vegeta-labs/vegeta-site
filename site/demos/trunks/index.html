Call the number you already hold

A bank details request opens a checklist: call the number on file, never a number from the email, and tell the old contact. After a cooling-off period a second person approves, and Trunks itself never changes bank details.

A payment message is not a bank change

Only when a strict list of conditions holds, such as a valid MT103 from an authenticated sender naming the deal's own accounts, does it not open a bank details check. Editing the type, a key value, a link or the risk replaces even approved checks.

Prepare the reply, a person sends it

Drafted from a template

The reply comes from a fixed template, filled from your records and from details a person confirmed, never from the words of the email. A person approves it, copies it into their own mail client, sends it and marks it sent; Trunks never sends email.

No recipient for an impersonator

The sender imitates a known contact, so the fixed acknowledgement has no allowed recipient and cannot be approved. Bank details, identity changes and every high-risk case only ever get this fixed reply. Internal words such as fraud or suspicious are blocked from any reply.
