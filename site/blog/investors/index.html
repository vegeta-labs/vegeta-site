    The route you take decides what you own, who votes, who can sell and what it costs, even when the company underneath is the same.

  • Angel investing with a day job: three questions to clear first

    Your job is why founders want you and also where your conflicts come from, so sort out what you know, what you control and what you have promised before you invest.

  • What an early-stage cheque buys: a percentage, some months and one question

    Pre-seed, seed and Series A are loose labels, so judge an early deal by your stake after dilution, the runway it pays for and what that runway will prove.

  • Late-stage deals: from headline valuation to the cash you receive

    A big valuation describes one class of share at one date, so work out what your own shares would receive at several sale prices before calling a price cheap.

  • Buying a venture stake second-hand: the price is the easy part

    A secondary hands you someone else's position, so budget for what comes with it, and look past the discount to what you are really taking on.

    A multiple shows that an investment made money. A public market equivalent shows whether the same cash, on the same dates, would have done better in an index.

  • What a fund's track record can and cannot tell you

    Past returns in venture carry real information, but only if you look at what was known when the decision was made.

  • What a portfolio headline does not tell you

  • How a venture fund of funds works, from both sides of the table

    A fund of funds trades an extra layer of cost for selection and access, which changes the sums for its investors and the conversation for the managers it backs.

  • Rolling funds: a subscription that adds exposure, not liquidity

    A rolling fund takes money each quarter, but what you own and when you can leave work very differently from a monthly subscription.

  • Investing with an independent sponsor: follow the fees and the carry

    Independent sponsors raise equity one acquisition at a time, and the same deal can pay the sponsor three different ways.
