Who may be shown a deal: UK financial promotion exemptions, person by person

Whether a lead may send a deal to someone depends on who that person is, how the message reaches them and what it says, and each exemption sets its own conditions.

Updated 27 November 2026

Explore

Bulma is about to send the Senzu Labs memo on 1 December 2026. Her list has sixty people on it. In the UK, a message that invites or encourages someone to invest, sent in the course of business, is a financial promotion. Bulma is not authorised by the FCA and nobody authorised has approved her memo, so every message needs an exemption in the Financial Promotion Order. The questions of whether she needs FCA permission at all, and what the high net worth and sophisticated investor statements say, are covered separately. This article goes through her list one person at a time.

How the message is sent matters

The Order sorts communications in two ways, and some exemptions only work for some kinds.

A real time communication is made in a personal visit, a phone call or another interactive dialogue. Anything else is non-real time, including letters, emails and anything published. A real time communication is solicited only if the recipient started the call or asked for it expressly, and it was clear the conversation would cover this kind of investment. Not saying no is not asking.

A communication can also be made to a person, such as an email to Goku, or directed at people generally, such as a post on a website. The Order treats causing a communication to be made as communicating it, so a memo forwarded at Bulma's request is still hers.

Bulma's list

Person Who they are Exemption that may fit What to check
Goku Signed a high net worth statement on 1 March 2026 High net worth individual, article 48 Statement signed in the 12 months ending on the day of sending; a qualifying investment; warning, sender details and the three required statements on the message
Piccolo Signed a self-certified sophisticated statement on 10 October 2025 Self-certified sophisticated investor, article 50A His statement is more than 12 months old on 1 December 2026, so it no longer counts
Krillin Works on investments at a venture firm Investment professional, article 19 Sent to him in that capacity, at his work address
Yamcha's family company A company with net assets of £6 million and three members High net worth company, article 49 Net assets or called-up share capital of at least £5 million, since it has 20 members or fewer
Tien Lives in Singapore Recipient outside the UK, article 12 Received outside the UK; an unsolicited call from the UK does not qualify
Chiaotzu No statement yet None for the memo Only ask whether he holds a current statement

Goku. The high net worth exemption can cover Bulma's email to Goku, or a call he asked for, but not a call she makes unprompted. It also covers only certain investments, such as shares or debt in an unlisted company, and only where the investor cannot be made to pay more than they commit; Senzu Labs' ordinary shares, held through the nominee, are assumed to meet that here. Bulma must believe on reasonable grounds that Goku is a high net worth individual, which is why she keeps his signed statement and its date.

The email must begin with the prescribed warning, in black bold type inside a black border, together with the sender's full name and an address for enquiries, and for a company its country of incorporation, registered office and number. It must also say that it is exempt because it is made to a high net worth individual, what the requirements are to qualify, and that anyone in doubt should consult an authorised person who specialises in advising on investments of this kind. A memo with the warning but without those statements is outside the exemption.

On a call he asked for, where a written warning is not practicable, the warning is given orally at the start, together with a statement that he will receive it in writing and that until then he should consider carefully any decision to invest. The written warning and information follow within two business days. The self-certified sophisticated exemption has corresponding warning, sender and statement requirements.

Piccolo. His statement was signed on 10 October 2025. The exemption looks at the 12 months ending with the day the communication is made, which for 1 December 2026 starts on 2 December 2025. His statement is outside that window. He needs to sign a new one before the memo goes to him, and sending it first and collecting the statement later does not work.

Krillin. Investment professionals include authorised firms and businesses whose ordinary activities involve the kind of activity the communication relates to. A director, officer or employee of one of them also counts, when the message is made to them in that capacity and their job involves the firm's investment activity. A memo sent to his work address, for his firm to consider, fits. A memo sent to his personal email about his own money is a different communication, and he would need his own statement like anyone else.

Yamcha's family company. For a company with more than 20 members, or a subsidiary of an undertaking with more than 20, the threshold is £500,000 of called-up share capital or net assets; otherwise it is £5 million. The test can also be met through another member of the company's group. The article also covers partnerships and unincorporated associations with net assets of at least £5 million, the trustees of trusts whose cash and investments, before liabilities, are £10 million or more or were at any time in the year before, and directors, officers and employees acting for those bodies in a role that involves their investment activity. Ask for the latest accounts, not a figure in an email.

Tien. A communication received outside the UK is generally exempt, but not if it is an unsolicited real time communication, unless it is made from outside the UK for a business carried on only outside the UK. A Singapore recipient also brings Singapore's rules with them, which this exemption says nothing about.

Chiaotzu. He cannot be sent the memo yet. Asking him whether he holds a current statement is a different thing, and the FCA's guidance is that an enquiry of that kind is not an inducement unless it involves persuasion. If he has one and sends it, the memo can follow under the exemption it supports.

Two more routes

Certified sophisticated investors. Article 50 needs more than a self-certification. The investor must hold a written certificate, signed by an authorised firm within the last three years, saying they understand the risks of the kind of investment involved, and must also have signed a statement within the last 12 months. The communication must not invite the investor to deal with the firm that signed the certificate, and it must relate only to the kind of investment the certificate covers and carry its own set of indications.

Reminders about the same deal. Where the first message was exempt because it carried the indications another article required, article 14 can exempt a follow-up. The follow-up must be non-real time or a solicited call, from the same sender, to a recipient of the first message, about the same activity and the same investment, and within 12 months of the recipient receiving the first one. A message about a different deal starts again.

What does not fit

A public social media post or a public web page is directed at people generally. A large group chat may be too, depending on who can join it and who reads it. The high net worth and self-certified exemptions are framed around a communication made to an individual whom the sender believes on reasonable grounds holds a statement, which a public post cannot meet. The FCA's guidance on financial promotions on social media says the rules apply on every channel and that unauthorised persons promoting without approval may be committing a criminal offence.

The other route is to have the content approved by an authorised firm. Since 7 February 2024, a firm can only approve another person's promotion if the FCA has given it permission to do so. Being authorised for something else is not enough. The exceptions include content prepared by the firm's own group, and by its appointed representative within the business the firm has accepted responsibility for.

Keep the record per message

For each send, keep who received it, the exemption relied on, the evidence for it and its date, and a copy of what was sent. Check the list against the evidence on the day of sending, because statements lapse on their own and the 12 months are counted back from that day.

In Bulma, Vegeta's SPV workspace, an investor's high net worth or sophisticated statement lasts 12 months, and a lead's message to investors is treated as a promotion: it opens with the risk warning and reaches only people who may see the deal that day. The legal texts it ships with are placeholders for the operator to replace.
