FAQ

About Vegeta

What is Vegeta?

Vegeta is open-source software for SPV and syndicate operations teams. Each module handles a specific part of the work. Goten — Deal Calculations reconciles receipts, works out the closing allocation and splits an exit, in your browser. Trunks — Operations Inbox prepares each email in your operations mailbox for a person to review, on your own server.

Is it free?

Yes. Both modules are open source under the Apache-2.0 licence. Goten needs no sign-up. For Trunks you pay only for your own server and, if you use one, a hosted model.

Is it a regulated service?

No. Vegeta is open-source software, not a regulated service. It does not hold money, give advice or act for you. A person checks every result and makes every decision.

Data and trust

Where does my data go?

Goten: Your deal data is processed in your browser and never sent by this app. The hosting provider still receives normal page requests. Trunks: it runs on your own server and sends only redacted text to the model provider you configure, or, with a local model, keeps the model traffic inside your network. What is sent to the model

How do the modules fit together?

They are separate tools. Trunks can import the deals, commitments, bank lines and reconciliation ledger you export from Goten as CSV files, read-only, and it suggests actions such as marking an investor withdrawn in Goten. A person makes the change.

How is it tested?

Each module page shows the figures from its latest release checks: Goten and Trunks. Both compare their rules with an independent Python implementation and use mutation checks. Trunks is also measured on emails that development never read, with a real model, once per release.
