# Investor guide: from an invitation to payment instructions

> Bulma v0.1.0. Source: https://github.com/vegeta-labs/bulma/blob/e0166a67c51edebce77f91d0d53c208b292cdebb/docs/en/use/investor-guide.md

You can see a deal only if you were invited, and only after a few checks. The exact steps, warnings and wording come from the operator who runs the instance, so yours may differ a little from this page.

## 1. Open your invitation

An invitation is a link from the lead, by email or by hand. Opening it shows **You have been invited** and a reference, but no details of the deal yet. **Before you start** says how many steps there are and roughly how long they take; times are estimates.

Then click **Sign in or create an account**. Your browser remembers the invitation for 30 days and adds it to your account when you sign in from it. An invitation sent to your email address works only for an account that confirmed that address.

If you see **This link does not work. Ask whoever sent it for a new one.**, the link is no longer usable.

## 2. Create an account

Click **Create an account**: first and last name, email, and a password of at least 12 characters. Open the link in the email and enter the same password to confirm your address. Two-step sign-in is optional for investors: turn it on under **Your account** with **Turn on two-step sign-in**.

## 3. Eligibility

On **Home**, the **Eligibility** card lists your steps in order, marked **(done)** or **(next)**. These are needed before you can see a deal. Depending on the operator, they include:

- **Statement** (called **Classification** under some operators): choose **High net worth**, **Sophisticated** or **None of these**, answer the questions and type your full name to sign. A qualifying statement lasts 12 months. If it does not qualify, you cannot continue for 12 months, unless the operator reopens it.
- **Assessment**: multiple-choice questions, and every answer must be right. You can try again, with different questions; after the second failed attempt, each further try waits 24 hours.
- **A risk warning** to read and confirm.
- **A waiting period** under some operators' rules: you click **Ask to see deals**, which starts a wait of at least 24 hours. You then read a **Before you go on** warning addressed to you and choose **Continue** or **Leave**, then go on with the statement and the assessment. When the wait is over, you choose **Continue** or **Leave** again.

## 4. Who you invest as

Under **Who you invest as**, in **Add one**, choose a **Type** (**Myself**, **A company**, **A trust** or **Another entity**) and click **Add**. Fill in the details and add proof files (PDF, PNG or JPEG). A company or trust also lists its people: directors, anyone with a share over 25% or in control, and whoever acts for it. Each beneficial owner confirms their details through their own link.

You can also add a **Bank account for refunds**. It must be in the entity's name, and a person at the operator checks it.

## 5. The deal page

Your deal appears under **Your deals**. The deal page starts with the operator's risk warning and then shows the company, **Key terms**, any **Custom terms**, the **Memo**, the pitch deck and **Documents**. Downloaded PDFs start with the warning.

Fees and investment amounts are marked as estimates. Only the amount to pay is exact.

To ask a question, use **Ask the lead**. The lead answers with a message: you read it on **Home** under **Messages from leads**. The email only says there is a new message. A message opens only while you may see the deal; otherwise it says to check your steps.

If you do not want to invest, click **I will not invest**.

## 6. Commit

Under **Invest**, type an amount and click **Show what I would pay**. You see how the amount to pay is made up, and estimates of your investment where the platform fee is shared at close. Choose **Who is investing** (and, if the deal offers one, a **Tax scheme**), then click **Commit** with the amount. You can commit before your identity check passes.

Each commitment has its own **Payment reference**. **Change the amount** works while the deal is live: up or down until money arrives, then only up. A change means signing again, and earlier payment details stop being valid. To withdraw after paying, see [Leaving a deal](#leaving-a-deal).

If the deal is full, **Show what I would pay** says why instead of offering **Commit**. If the lead keeps a waiting list, click **Join the waiting list for** the amount. Nothing is created for you to pay while you wait. The **Waiting list** card shows **Waiting** or **Let in**; the lead decides who is let in, in no set order, and you are told. Once let in, commit as usual; whether there is room is checked again when you commit. **Leave the waiting list** takes you off it.

## 7. Identity check

You can send the check as soon as the entity is added, even before you commit. It must pass before you sign.

When nothing is missing, tick **This information is true** and click **Send for review, no face scan**. On a real instance with an identity provider you may also see **Check me through the provider**. Either way, a person at the operator decides. The status shows next to the entity:

**To do**, **With the provider**, **In review**, **Passed**, **Sent back** (with the reason), **Expired** or **Cannot be used**.

A pass lasts 3 years unless the operator set otherwise. Changing the entity's details sends it back to be checked.

## 8. Sign

Click **Deal sheet** once your identity check has passed. Read it, tick **I have read this document and agree to sign it.**, type your full name and click **Sign**. Some deal sheets are signed as a deed outside the site; the operator sends those to you. You can always download the **Signed deal sheet**.

## 9. Pay

Click **Payment details**. The page shows **Still to pay**, your **Payment reference**, the **Pay by** date, the **Receiving account** and a **Your money** panel with what the operator states about the account. The software has not checked it.

- **This software does not receive money. Pay only to the account shown, which the operator set up.**
- Type the details from this page, not from a saved payee.
- Pay from an account in the name of whoever invests, in the deal's currency.
- Before paying, use **Check these details** to see they are still valid. Account changes are never sent by email.

Then click **I have paid**. This tells the lead; it is not a receipt. A person at the operator records money when it arrives.

The page also says what to ask your bank for if a payment seems stuck. If you paid with the wrong reference, click **I used the wrong reference**, say the date, the amount, the name on your account and the reference you used, and click **Tell the operator**. A person at the operator checks the payment and gets in touch.

## Your statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Verifying** | Your identity check has not passed yet. |
| **To sign** | Sign the deal sheet. |
| **To pay** | Payment details are ready. |
| **Payment sent** | You said you have paid. |
| **Part paid** | Some of the amount has been recorded as received. |
| **Funded** | The full amount has been recorded. |
| **Funded · refund due** | More than the amount has been recorded. |
| **Reconfirm terms** | The terms changed: agree or decline. |
| **Confirm amount** | The lead proposed a new amount. |
| **Removed** / **Withdrawn** / **Cancelled** | Your commitment has ended. |
| **Not approved** | The check on who you invest as was refused, so that commitment ended. Any money you paid is held while the operator deals with it. |

## When the terms change

If the lead changes the deal after you committed, the deal page shows **What changed since you committed**: the lead's sentence and what changed. Some changes are only for your information.

If you must reconfirm, you see **The terms have changed**, with the differences and the last day to answer, counted in working days. Tick **I have read the changes and agree to them** and click **Agree**. If your deal sheet changed, agreeing means signing it again with your full name. To decline, tick the box that says your commitment ends and click **Do not agree**; any money you sent is refunded. Silence is not agreement: if you do not answer in time, the operator may end your commitment.

## When the lead proposes a new amount

The lead can propose a new amount while no money has arrived from you. Your deal page shows **The lead proposes a new amount**: what you pay now and what you would pay, how the new amount is made up, and the last day to answer. Your earlier payment details are no longer valid once you agree.

To accept, tick **I agree to the new amount** and click **Agree**; if your deal sheet needs it, you then sign it again. To say no, click **Keep my amount**. If you do not answer in time, your amount stays as it is. If the deal no longer has room for a higher amount, the page says so and you can only keep your amount.

## Leaving a deal

There is no button to withdraw. Write to the lead.

- If no money has arrived from you, the lead can remove you.
- If money has arrived, the lead records your written request, and a reviewer at the operator accepts it. Your commitment ends and the operator refunds you.

A deal can also be cancelled. Then every commitment ends and money received is refunded. A person at the operator sends refunds from the bank, normally to the account the money came from, and a second person approves each one. The app does not show a refund's progress.

## Mail you get

In the sandbox no mail is sent. On a real instance you get short emails when something needs you or has happened: your commitment was recorded, money was received, your identity check passed or needs more, payment and reconfirmation reminders, a new message, a changed deal, or a proposed new amount. They hold no details of the deal: sign in to see them.

A mail whose title starts with **Do not send money** or **Do not pay** is urgent: do not pay with the details you have. Open **Payment details** again before you pay. **Your documents** on **Home** keeps your signed deal sheet and any payment details that are still valid.

## Complaints

On **Home**, **Complaints** shows where a complaint can go. Click **Make a complaint**, write it and click **Send the complaint**. The page says by when it will be acknowledged.
