Trunks

Use Trunks

v0.4.0

Trunks is the operations inbox of Vegeta. For each email that arrives in a shared operations mailbox, it picks a category, pulls out the key facts, links the email to deals and people, flags risk and prepares a reply. You check the reply, copy it and send it from your own mail client.

These pages are for operations staff. You do not need to install anything or write code. An admin in your team has to set Trunks up first.

A person remains responsible for every reply and every risk decision. A low risk rating is not a clearance.

Start here#

  1. Walk the demo: sign in, read the inbox, deal with one case from start to finish and see a bank details check.

Daily work#

  • Read a case: category, key details, links to deals and people, and risk.
  • Check and send a reply: check a prepared reply, copy it, send it from your own mail client and close the case.
  • Risk and verification: risk signals, verification tasks, two-person control and when a case can be closed.

Who does what#

  • Roles: what a reviewer, an approver and an admin can each do.

When you need help#

  • Limits and help: what Trunks does not do, what to do when it is wrong or unavailable, and who to ask.
  • After an upgrade to v0.4.0: approved replies and verification tasks that must be done again.

If you deploy or administer Trunks, see the administrator documentation.
