Policy

Examples, your data and advice.

Examples

The demos, pictures and worked examples on this site use fictional deals, companies, people and emails.

Your data

Goten: Your deal data is processed in your browser and never sent by this app. The hosting provider still receives normal page requests.

Trunks: it runs on your own server and sends only redacted text to the model provider you configure, or, with a local model, keeps the model traffic inside your network.

Bulma: it runs on the operator’s own server, which keeps investors’ details and documents; if the operator uses Sumsub, Sumsub holds what it collects under the operator’s contract.

Explore

On Explore, matching while you type happens in your browser. When you press Find pages, what you typed is sent through Vercel’s AI Gateway to the model provider chosen for this site, only to pick pages of this site. This site does not store or log it, and asks the gateway to use only providers that do not train on it.

To limit use, the site counts requests by a keyed hash of your IP address; the count for the minute is deleted after a minute and the count for the day after 26 hours. Vercel, which hosts this site, keeps normal request records, including IP addresses, for a short time.

The suggestions are links to pages of this site, not advice about your situation.

Not advice

This is not investment, tax or legal advice. Check every result yourself before you rely on it. Not affiliated with any platform.

Vegeta is open-source software, not a regulated service. It does not hold money or give advice.
